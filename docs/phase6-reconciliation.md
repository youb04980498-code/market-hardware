# Phase 6 reconciliation

Every new source-content release compares independently calculated Python totals from the same repeatable-read source snapshot against Spark Gold read back from committed Delta. Checks include all orders, paid orders, captured gross, successful/inferred historical refunds, net revenue, all line quantities, captured sold units, product-attributed gross/refund/net, stock quantities/reservations and customer registry count. Every difference must be zero before publishing a pointer. Negative net/product balances or refunds exceeding captures stop publication. Source stock and currency guards reject impossible balances and unsupported currency.

The audit's initial real snapshot contained 172 orders, 137 captured orders and 122 currently paid orders. Captures were 4,155,700 cents, refunds 531,400 cents and net 3,624,300 cents. Source inventory and test history are retained; later tests legitimately add isolated orders/payments/refunds. These are synthetic local figures, not real sales. The execution report records final measured totals and a real quarterly example.

Payments are counted once by payment business identity, not by count of lifecycle Kafka events or financial audit records. Every raw Kafka delivery is retained separately. Duplicate event IDs cannot increase revenue because revenue is reconciled from current authoritative payment/refund entities. Old REFUNDED fixtures require status-based full-refund inference, explicitly labelled; unknown historical refund dates are never invented.

For a discrepancy, inspect the release ID, snapshot boundary and each check's source/analytical/difference fields through `/api/analytics/pipeline/status`. Restore source services and queue another backfill. Do not edit payment history, manually mark outboxes or overwrite Delta rows to force agreement. Product monetary allocation deliberately preserves whole-order cents with deterministic cumulative integer division.

Quarterly test procedure: materialize a selected calendar quarter twice and compare identical release/results; create a real isolated successful checkout, publish its capture, apply a real partial refund through Payments, verify unchanged gross and exact net reduction, then fully refund and rerun its original quarter. A corrected report must retain gross, increase refunds by the remaining amount and have a new source-content release. Tests retain these audit records.
